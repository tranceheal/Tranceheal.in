What is hypnotherapy, and how does it work on tranceheal.in? ▼

Hypnotherapy (सम्मोहन थेरेपी) is a natural, gentle, and deeply relaxing process where a guided practitioner helps you quiet your active conscious mind and connect with your subconscious mind (Avachetan Man). In hypnotherapy, we use soothing vocal guidance and relaxation techniques to bring your brain into a calm Theta meditative state. You can gently release old negative thought patterns, dissolve overthinking, and plant empowering suggestions for confidence, peace, and healthy habits.

Is hypnotherapy safe, and can I get stuck in a trance? ▼

Yes, hypnotherapy is 100% natural, safe, and pleasant. You experience natural trance states every single day—such as when daydreaming or getting lost in music. You CANNOT get stuck in hypnosis. If the practitioner stops speaking, you will either naturally open your eyes after a few quiet moments or drift into a refreshing nap and wake up. You remain in complete control throughout the entire session.

What can I expect during a session with TranceHeal? ▼

A standard session has 3 comfortable phases: (1) Friendly Conversation (20 mins) to discuss your goals; (2) Guided Trance & Healing (40–45 mins) reclining comfortably with eyes closed to release emotional knots; (3) Gentle Grounding (10–15 mins) returning to waking awareness feeling light and peaceful, with a personalized home audio anchor provided for daily listening.

How many sessions are typically needed to see real changes? ▼

For specific challenges like smoking cessation, stage fear, or isolated overthinking, clients often experience profound relief within 1 to 2 sessions. For deeper patterns like chronic insomnia, unresolved past memories, or comprehensive inner transformation, 3 to 4 sessions are recommended.
